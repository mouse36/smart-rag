# SunnyMentor: AI-Powered Support for Selective Mutism Families

## Executive Summary

**SunnyMentor** is an AI-powered virtual assistant designed to provide immediate, accessible, and scientifically-grounded support to families dealing with Selective Mutism (SM). Our platform bridges the critical gap between professional consultation availability and urgent family needs, offering 24/7 guidance through an intelligent chatbot interface.

---

## 1. Project Concept

### Core Value Proposition
SunnyMentor addresses a critical unmet need in the Selective Mutism community by providing:
- **Immediate Access**: 24/7 availability when professional help isn't accessible
- **Evidence-Based Guidance**: Scientific knowledge distilled into practical advice
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Bilingual support (English/Chinese) for diverse families
- **Professional Quality**: Expert-level knowledge without the cost barrier

### Market Opportunity
- **Target Market**: Parents and caregivers of children with Selective Mutism
- **Market Size**: Estimated 1-2% of children worldwide affected by SM
- **Current Gap**: Limited access to specialized professionals, especially in underserved areas
- **Solution**: AI-powered virtual coach providing immediate, reliable guidance

### Mission & Vision
- **Mission**: Democratize access to Selective Mutism expertise through AI technology
- **Vision**: Become the go-to digital resource for SM families worldwide, ensuring no family faces SM challenges alone

## 2. Product Design Process

**Primary Job**: "Help me understand and support my child with Selective Mutism right now, when I need it most"

**Supporting Jobs**:
