SunnyMentor ｜Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What kinds of questions can SunnyMentor answer?

SunnyMentor is particularly helpful with:

  • Basic knowledge and common misconceptions about Selective Mutism
  • Practical strategies for parents to use at home
  • Guidance for teachers on supporting children at school
  • Coping with related challenges like social anxiety or adjustment difficulties
  • Information about SunnyMindED programs and services

2. Is the information provided by Sunny Knows reliable?

Sunny Knows is built on a rich knowledge base, including:

  • Authoritative books such as The Selective Mutism Resource Manual, The Practical Handbook for Selective Mutism, and The Parent & Teacher Guide to SM
  • Intervention case studies and educational materials developed by the SunnyMindED expert team
  • Psychological research and clinical insights from both Chinese and international sources

⚠️ Please Note: All responses are generated by artificial intelligence and are for reference only. They do not replace medical advice or personalized consultation. If your situation is urgent or complex, please consult a qualified professional.

3. Does SunnyMentor support both Chinese and English?

Yes, SunnyMentor understands both Chinese and English.
You can ask questions in Simplified Chinese or English. Bilingual families are welcome to use either language.

4. Is there a cost to use SunnyMentor?

A free demo version is currently available.
Full features are accessible to registered users. Please log in or follow the on-screen instructions to subscribe and gain full access.

5. Will my private questions be saved?

We take your privacy seriously.
Chat history data are saved in your account so that you can access your previous chats later. All question data is used only to improve the AI assistant and will never be shared or sold.
If you prefer additional privacy, you may ask questions anonymously or request direct support from a human expert.

6. What if I don't get a satisfactory answer?

SunnyMentor is continuously improving. If your answer feels:

  • Off-topic or misaligned with your question
  • Too general or lacking detail
  • Repetitive or awkward in language

Please feel free to rephrase your question or contact our team for tailored support.

8. Who is SunnyMentor designed for?

This tool is ideal for:

  • Parents supporting a child recovering from Selective Mutism
  • Educators working with SM children
  • Anyone interested in learning effective SM intervention strategies
  • Current SunnyMindED clients and coaching program participants
